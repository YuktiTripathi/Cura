# Evidence map, revision 2

## Claim level evidence map

Every claim in the revised sample topics is listed below with the source page I read in full during this session. Claims that no reliable page supported were rewritten to say only what the source says, or removed; those changes are listed after the table. **Supported** means the source page states the claim as written in the sample. **Product information** means it comes from MetSights, not from medical evidence. Nothing here is clinical approval: every claim stays pending clinical review until a clinician signs it off.

## What changed after reading the full source pages

| Earlier wording | Problem | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The placenta makes ALP in pregnancy | No reliable page found that says this for lay readers | Rewritten: ALP is normally higher during pregnancy |
| Low vitamin D raises ALP through faster bone turnover | The mechanism was not stated by the source | Rewritten: long low vitamin D can soften bones (osteomalacia), one cause of high ALP |
| Folate causes the same large red cell change | Not stated in the sources read | Removed as a claim; the text now says only that a doctor may also check folate |
| Anaemia makes HbA1c misleading | Too broad; NIDDK names specific factors | Rewritten to the NIDDK list: very low iron, blood loss, kidney failure, haemoglobin variants |
| Low HbA1c on diabetes medicines means sugar drops too low | No source found | Removed; the member is told to ask their doctor what a low result means for them |
| Working muscles take glucose out of the blood | Mechanism not stated by the sources read | Rewritten to the CDC and NIDDK wording |
| CRP rises and falls faster than ESR | Not stated in the sources read | Rewritten: CRP rises and falls with inflammation and is often ordered with ESR |
| New headache with scalp tenderness over 50 | Specific detail not in the source read | Rewritten to the MedlinePlus reasons for testing |
| Herbal, Ayurvedic or bodybuilding supplements | Named one tradition without a source | Rewritten to the LiverTox wording on herbal and dietary supplements |
| Aim for 150 minutes of activity a week for fatty liver | NIDDK gives no amount for fatty liver | Removed; replaced with the NIDDK statement that activity helps even without weight loss |
| Poor sleep makes metabolism less efficient | No source found | Rewritten to the NIDDK insulin resistance wording |
